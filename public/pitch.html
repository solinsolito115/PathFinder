The gap

The bill and the payment do not live in the same place.

Custody is a document

A bill of lading can be copied, delayed, or argued over. It cannot hold the funds.

Cash waits on a wire

Release depends on email and a second approval, not on the fact of delivery.

Sensors never settle

A temperature spike starts a dispute. It does not change what the carrier is paid.

The instrument

A bill of lading that holds the money.

The shipper locks escrow when the bill is issued. The program pays when the consignee signs. No second signature.
